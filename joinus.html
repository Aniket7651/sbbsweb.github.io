Join Us

Any person of good character or well-wisher of humanity can become member of the Society. Any person who has interest in the discipline of Bioinformatics and Biological sciences can become the member of the society. Life Members shall have voting right. Interested person shall apply on prescribed form and recommendation of Executive council is mandatory for becoming a Life member. Life member shall pay prescribed fee. Promises to abide by the rules and bye-laws and all the decisions taken by the Society in respect of the amendments of the rules and bye-laws. Subscribed in writing to carry out to the best of his/her capacity the objects of the Society.

There shall be four kinds of membership
  • General member: Person who pays Rs. 800/- as yearly subscription.
  • Life member: Person who pays Rs. 7000/- as subscription.
  • Honorary member: Person who is scientist, educationist, industrialist and good worker of philanthropy may be appointed as honorary member of the Society.
  • For Undergraduate and Postgraduate students:Rs. 500/- as yearly subscription.
